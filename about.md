## Why “Jake’s Junk Drawer”?

Jake’s Junk Drawer is meant to be a project notebook.

The name is a little playful, but the purpose is serious: it is a place to organize the ideas, projects, and experiments I build while trying to understand markets, models, data, and complex systems.

Some posts are polished writeups. Others are more like research notes from the middle of the process.
