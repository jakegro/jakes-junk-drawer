# About

Hi, I’m Jake.

I studied Physics and Statistics at Villanova and am continuing into data science. I’m interested in quant finance, statistical modeling, machine learning, and projects where math meets real-world uncertainty.

This site is called **Jake’s Junk Drawer** because I want it to be a place for useful, weird, unfinished, and interesting ideas. Some posts will be polished projects. Others may be notes from things I am still figuring out.
